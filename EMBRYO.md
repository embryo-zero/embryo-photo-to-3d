# Embryo Photo to 3D

Embryo Photo to 3D is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Meshroom](https://github.com/alicevision/Meshroom) (nightly, commit d787a6b). It does 3D models from photos.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING.md, LICENSE-MPL2.md) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
